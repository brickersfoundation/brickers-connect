Brickers Connect — Product Prototype

This interactive concept demonstrates a possible connection platform for The Brickers Foundation. Organization descriptions and geography are based on public Brickers Foundation features where available. Match counts, analytics, and dashboard metrics are illustrative demo data. Community Blog submissions publish to the live Brickers Connect database. Submitter contact email stays private and is visible only through the protected Admin view.
